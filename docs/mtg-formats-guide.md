# Magic: The Gathering — Game Formats Reference Guide

## A Note on Ratios and Mana Curves

Throughout this guide, you'll find suggested card counts and mana curve targets for each format. These are guidelines, not mandates. Every deck has a unique goal, and the right mix of lands, spells, creatures, and interaction depends entirely on how you plan to win. A fast aggro deck wants a very different curve than a control deck or a combo build. Use these numbers as your starting point — then tune relentlessly toward your win condition.

---

## Part I: Constructed Formats

### Standard
- **Deck Size:** Minimum 60 cards (60 strongly recommended)
- **Sideboard:** Up to 15 cards
- **Copies:** Maximum 4 of any card except basic lands
- **Card Pool:** Most recent 2–3 years of sets; rotates each fall
- **Life Total:** 20
- **Play:** Best-of-three; 20 life

| Category | Range | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Lands | 22–26 | Aggro: 22–23. Control: 25–26. |
| Creatures | 12–24 | Aggro runs more; control/combo fewer or none. |
| Spells / Removal | 8–16 | Interaction, burn, counters. Tune to meta. |
| Card Draw | 4–8 | Keeps hand full and finds win condition. |
| Win Conditions | 4–8 | Combo pieces or finishers; may overlap with creatures. |

### Pioneer
- **Deck Size:** Minimum 60 cards
- **Sideboard:** Up to 15 cards
- **Copies:** Maximum 4 except basic lands
- **Card Pool:** Return to Ravnica (Oct 2012) forward; all 10 original fetch lands are banned
- **Life Total:** 20; best-of-three

| Category | Range | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Lands | 22–26 | Dual lands and mana fixing are available and important. |
| Creatures | 8–24 | Depends heavily on archetype. |
| Interaction / Removal | 8–14 | Efficient, often 1–2 mana answers. |
| Card Advantage | 4–8 | Planeswalkers and draw spells are strong here. |
| Combo Pieces / Finishers | 4–8 | Pioneer rewards consistent combo lines. |

### Modern
- **Deck Size:** Minimum 60 cards
- **Sideboard:** Up to 15 cards
- **Copies:** Maximum 4 except basic lands
- **Card Pool:** Eighth Edition (July 2003) forward
- **Life Total:** 20; best-of-three; games decided turns 3–5

| Category | Range | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Lands | 19–24 | Fetch + shocklands enable 3–4 color reliably. |
| Creatures | 8–20 | Efficient threats; many at 1–2 mana. |
| Interaction | 10–16 | Lightning Bolt, Path to Exile, Fatal Push are staples. |
| Card Advantage / Filters | 4–8 | Looting, cantrips, planeswalkers. |
| Combo / Payoff Cards | 4–12 | Highly variable by archetype. |

⚠️ Modern rewards tight curves. Cards costing 4+ mana must win the game or generate overwhelming value.

### Legacy
- **Deck Size:** Minimum 60 cards; all sets ever printed minus banned list
- **Life Total:** 20; games can end turn 1–2

| Category | Range | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Lands | 17–22 | Fetch + duals provide consistent mana even at low counts. |
| Free Spells | 4–8 | Force of Will, Daze, Lotus Petal. |
| Interaction | 10–16 | Hyper-efficient; 1-mana answers to everything. |
| Threats | 8–16 | Often 1–2 mana with immediate impact. |
| Combo / Engine Pieces | 8–16 | Many Legacy decks are combo-centric. |

### Vintage
- **Deck Size:** Minimum 60 cards; maximum 4 of non-restricted cards; restricted cards limited to 1 copy
- **Restricted List includes:** Power Nine (Black Lotus, all 5 Moxen, Ancestral Recall, Time Walk, Timetwister), Demonic Tutor, Tinker, Yawgmoth's Will, Gush, and many others

## Part II: Limited Formats

### Booster Draft
- **Deck Size:** Minimum 40 cards; lands provided by event
- **Sideboard:** All remaining drafted cards
- **Players:** 8; each opens 3 packs, picks 1 card and passes

| Category | Range | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Lands | 17 | Near-universal standard for 40-card limited. |
| Creatures | 13–16 | Creatures win games in limited. |
| Removal / Spells | 6–9 | Removal is premium; include all you can. |
| Bombs / Win Conditions | 2–4 | High-rarity cards that swing the game. |

⚠️ 17 lands is almost always correct in draft. Curve peaks at 3–4 mana. Limit 6+ CMC cards to 1–2 unless you have ramp.

### Sealed
- **Deck Size:** Minimum 40 cards from 6 opened booster packs
- Same ratios as Draft: 17 lands, 13–16 creatures, 6–9 spells. Prioritize mana fixing if splashing a third color.

## Part III: Multiplayer & Casual Formats

### Commander (EDH) ← PRIMARY FORMAT
- **Deck Size:** Exactly 100 cards including the commander
- **Commander:** 1 legendary creature in the command zone
- **Singleton:** Only 1 copy of each card except basic lands
- **Color Identity:** All cards must match commander's color identity
- **Life Total:** 40; typically 3–4 players
- **Commander Damage:** 21 combat damage from one commander eliminates a player
- **Commander Tax:** +2 colorless each time it's recast from command zone

**Suggested Ratios (99 cards + commander):**

| Category | Range | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Lands | 36–38 | 38 for high-curve; 36 with strong ramp and low curve. |
| Ramp | 10–12 | Sol Ring, mana rocks, Cultivate, Kodama's Reach, land fetchers. |
| Card Draw / Advantage | 10–12 | Most under-included category. Draw = winning. |
| Removal | 8–12 | ~7–9 spot removal + 2–3 board wipes. |
| Threats / Win Conditions | 28–34 | Your gameplan: creatures, combos, synergy pieces. |

**Simple starting formula:** 36 lands + 10 ramp + 10 draw + 10 removal + 33 threats/synergy = 99

⚠️ These ratios are a starting framework, not a formula. A storm combo deck might run 12+ tutors and almost no creatures. A Voltron deck might run 20+ auras and equipment. Always optimize for your win condition. Ask: does this card help me win?

### Brawl
- **Deck Size:** Exactly 60 cards including commander
- **Commander:** 1 legendary creature or planeswalker
- **Card Pool:** Standard-legal only; rotates with Standard
- **Life Total:** 25 (1v1) or 30 (multiplayer)

| Category | Range | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Lands | 24–26 | Higher than constructed; singleton means less consistency. |
| Ramp | 6–8 | Fewer options than Commander but still critical. |
| Card Draw | 6–8 | Crucial for finding commander synergies. |
| Removal / Interaction | 6–8 | Spot removal and 1–2 board wipes. |
| Threats / Synergy | 16–20 | Cards that work with your commander's strategy. |

### Historic Brawl (MTG Arena)
- **Deck Size:** Exactly 100 cards including commander; 1v1 on Arena; 25 life
- **Card Pool:** All Historic-legal cards on Arena (includes Alchemy and Remastered)
- Same ratios as Commander but optimized for 1v1: more targeted removal, fewer political cards, consistency matters more.

### Pauper
- **Deck Size:** Minimum 60 cards; sideboard up to 15
- **Card Pool:** Any card ever printed at common rarity
- **Life Total:** 20; best-of-three; blue control, red burn, and mono-black removal dominate

## Part IV: Mana Curve Reference

The Golden Rule: **your mana curve should serve your win condition**, not the other way around.

| Format | Ideal Curve Peak | Max Curve | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 2–4 mana | 5–7 mana | 5+ mana cards need to dominate the board. |
| Pioneer | 2–3 mana | 4–5 mana | Format is fast; late-game cards need to end it. |
| Modern | 1–3 mana | 4 mana | Every 4+ mana card must win or generate +3 cards in value. |
| Legacy / Vintage | 0–2 mana | 3–4 mana | Free spells and fast mana accelerate dramatically. |
| Draft / Sealed | 3–4 mana | 6–7 (2 max) | Games go longer; 1–2 bombs expected. |
| Commander | 3–4 mana | 6–10 mana | Longer games justify high-cost spells; ramp is essential. |
| Brawl / Hist. Brawl | 2–4 mana | 6–8 mana | 1v1 rewards faster curves than multiplayer Commander. |

**When evaluating a card, ask:**
1. Does it advance my gameplan?
2. Can I cast it consistently?
3. Does it replace itself (card advantage)?
4. What turn do I want to win — does my curve support that?
5. Is this the best card for this slot?
